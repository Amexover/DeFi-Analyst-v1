# Data and research rights

[LICENSE](LICENSE) applies to the original software and its software documentation.

DefiLlama, CoinGecko and other upstream sources retain their respective rights
in their data and documentation. The software license does not relicense
third-party API responses or grant additional redistribution rights.

The offline demo uses synthetic values, explicitly labelled as a fixture.
They are not quotes or measurements of a real protocol.

Reports or datasets created using this service need their own attribution and
reuse decisions. Record source URLs, observation dates, retrieval times and
relevant provider terms when preparing research for publication.

See [data methodology](docs/data-methodology.md) for interpretation limits.
